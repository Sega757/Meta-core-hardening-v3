# Audits

External and internal reviews of the META-CORE architecture. Unlike
[`docs/decisions/`](../decisions/README.md), an audit here is not itself a
decision — it's a recorded evaluation, critique, or counter-proposal that
may or may not lead to a future ADR. Adopting any recommendation from an
audit should still go through a normal ADR.
